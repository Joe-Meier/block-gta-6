# Block GTA 6

One button. It hides every LinkedIn post and comment that mentions GTA 6.

The site is a single `index.html`. The big "Block GTA 6" link is a bookmarklet: drag it to your bookmarks bar, open LinkedIn, click it.

It catches "GTA 6", "GTA6", "GTA VI", "GTA Six" and "Grand Theft Auto VI" in any capitalization, and leaves "GTA 5" and "GTA Vice City" alone. It runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. Hidden posts come back when you reload LinkedIn; click it again to keep blocking.

Not affiliated with LinkedIn or Rockstar Games.
